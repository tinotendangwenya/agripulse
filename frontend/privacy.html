Privacy notice

AgriPulse is run by [organisation name]. Questions: [contact email or phone].

What we keep
  • Your account: name, email and/or phone, your role, and your password. The password is stored only as a one-way scrambled value, so nobody (including us) can read it.
  • What you add: farms, crops, harvests, money and animal records, listings, orders, purchase requests, price quotes and alerts, transport jobs, equipment and bookings.
  • Questions to experts: your question, your replies and any photos you send.
  • Notifications sent to you inside the app, and a record of the time you accepted this notice.
  • An audit record of admin actions on accounts (for example a password reset), so mistakes and misuse can be traced.
Who can see what
  • Everyone who is logged in can see your name and your province or district on listings, requests, offers, transport jobs and equipment you post. Price quotes you send show only your role (for example "a registered buyer"), never your name.
  • Agricultural experts see your name, your farm's name and province, your question and any photos you attach. They do not see your phone number or email.
  • Phone numbers and emails are not shown to other users. The exception: when a transport quote is accepted or an equipment booking is confirmed, the two people involved can see each other's phone and email so they can arrange the job.
  • Administrators can see account details (name, email, phone, role), expert applications, and can reset passwords or switch accounts off.
Photos

Photos you send to an expert are stored on our server. They are shown only to you and to experts, and are not sent to any other company. Your phone shrinks them before sending. They are kept until we delete them by hand. There is no delete button yet: ask us if you want them removed.

Other services that see some information
  • Weather: to give a forecast we send your farm's coordinates (or the centre of your province if you gave none) to Open-Meteo. We do not send your name.
  • Page files: your browser loads the Bootstrap and Chart.js code from a public network (jsDelivr). That network can see that your browser asked for those files.
How long we keep things

In-app alerts are deleted automatically: read ones after 30 days, all of them after 90 days. Everything else is kept until your data is removed.

Your choices
  • You can change your password, or deactivate your account, under Account after you log in. Deactivating stops anyone logging in as you but does not erase your records.
  • To have your data corrected or erased, contact [contact email or phone].

AgriPulse does not take payments. Prices, advice and estimates in the app are guides, not guarantees.
